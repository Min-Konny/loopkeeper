# Localization coverage and contracts

The unpublished candidate supports Japanese (`ja`, default), English (`en`), Simplified Chinese (`zh-CN`) and Korean (`ko`). Each dictionary owns all3383 registered keys with matching named placeholders and no untranslated fallback in the accepted scope. All183 archived version titles and476 bullets have English, Simplified Chinese and Korean translations. `zh-Hant` remains a distinct planned locale; no additional language has been selected.

The separate `loopkeeper-ui-locale-v1` preference stores the UI locale independently of progress. It is not a game ID, achievement condition, save-schema field or reset/migration input. A language change is saved immediately. Failed preference writes retain the previous locale. Switching language preserves typed and empty drafts, focus, legal scroll position, reservations and prior pause/running intent. Gameplay formulas, rewards, difficulty and serialized save compatibility remain unchanged.

Known definitions are translated through stable IDs and exact verified source matches. Translated names are never used as model lookup keys. User names, unknown saved descriptions, historical journals and opaque enemy-report names without stable IDs remain verbatim; they are not guessed or rewritten. New log presentation metadata is ephemeral and adds no serialized fields. Missing future Chinese/Korean entries fall back to English, and wholly missing keys remain visible as `[key]`; no current3383 entry relies on that fallback. Locale completeness checks titles and every archived bullet before marking an archive article complete.

Text is escaped or inserted as text, never evaluated as translated markup. `Intl` handles locale presentation; stored numbers, identifiers, ISO export names and dates keep their original meaning. Bundled derivative Loopkeeper SC/KR fonts have their original SIL Open Font License notices alongside them. Unchanged third-party assets and notices from the public baseline are retained. No runtime dependency or external translation/API service was added.

Functional acceptance covers the documented known current screens and finite synthetic old-format save variants. Final Korean regression:14 tests and99 browser observations at1280/390/320 widths, including archive, selection/reload, pause/close, main/current/legacy flows, arbitrary-prose preservation and running input/focus/scroll. Earlier English/Chinese and interaction checkpoints remain preserved; this packaging step does not repeat heavy gameplay testing.

This is AI-authored and AI-reviewed localization. Independent native-speaker linguistic review and physical-device/OS/IME acceptance have not been performed. Desktop Chromium mobile viewport checks do not substitute for physical phones or every possible save combination. Published archived notes describe their historical version scope, including past translation limitations. The still-unpublished0.6.140 entry is aligned with this final candidate in all four locales; its earlier partial-English draft is preserved in prior checkpoints. The current source and final acceptance record supersede older partial-localization handoff documents.
